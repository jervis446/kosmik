Kosmik in Claude and ChatGPT

Add Kosmik to Claude or ChatGPT and ask about your chart there. Your AI reads your exact kundli, dashas and transits from Kosmik instead of guessing, then explains them in its own words.

Before you start

  1. Have a Kosmik account, and add your birth date, time and place in Kosmik (Account → My birth details). The chart tools read them from there.
  2. Keep this connector address handy. You’ll paste it in the next step.
https://kosmik-mcp.prime-adarsh108.workers.dev/mcp

Add it to Claude

  1. On claude.ai or the Claude desktop app, open Settings → Connectors.
  2. Click Add custom connector. Name it Kosmik and paste the connector address. Click Add.
  3. Kosmik now appears in your connectors list. Click it, then click Connect.
  4. A Kosmik page opens. Sign in with your Asanoha account if asked, then tap Allow. You’re sent back to Claude.
  5. In a chat, make sure Kosmik is turned on in the tools menu, and ask away. Once connected, it works in Claude’s phone app too.

Added but it isn’t working? It’s probably added but not connected yet: open Kosmik in the connectors list and click Connect. If no Kosmik page opens, allow pop-ups for claude.ai.

Add it to ChatGPT

  1. On chatgpt.com, open Settings → Apps & Connectors. If you don’t see a way to add your own, turn on Developer mode under Advanced settings.
  2. Click Create. Name it Kosmik, paste the connector address as the MCP server URL, and choose OAuth for authentication.
  3. Click Create, then sign in with your Asanoha account on the Kosmik page and tap Allow.
  4. In a new chat, pick Kosmik from the + menu and ask your question.

Adding your own connectors depends on your ChatGPT plan and region. If the option isn’t there, Claude is the easiest way to use Kosmik today.

Your details stay yours

Kosmik only reads: it never changes your account. Claude or ChatGPT sees your birth details and chart only when it calls a tool. You can disconnect any time in their settings, and delete your details in Kosmik whenever you like. Privacy Policy

Problems?

I added Kosmik but it doesn’t show as connected.

Adding saves the connector; connecting signs you in. Open Kosmik in the connectors list and click Connect, then Allow on the Kosmik page.

The Kosmik sign-in page didn’t open.

Your browser probably blocked the pop-up. Allow pop-ups for claude.ai or chatgpt.com and click Connect again.

It says to add my birth details first.

Open Kosmik, go to Account → My birth details and save your date, time and place of birth. Then ask again.

It says I’ve used today’s lookups.

Free accounts get 11 lookups a day and Premium gets 51. They refill at midnight India time.

How do I disconnect?

In Claude or ChatGPT, open the connector settings, choose Kosmik and disconnect or remove it. Kosmik stops answering it right away.
