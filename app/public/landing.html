How it behaves

Guidance, not fear.

Astrology, tarot and palmistry are spiritual guidance and entertainment. These rules are built into every answer.

No scary predictions

It never predicts death, illness, accidents or guaranteed outcomes. A life line says nothing about lifespan.

No fear-based selling

At most three suggestions, only when relevant, always optional. No “dosha will ruin you”.

No invented verses

Scripture answers quote retrieved verses only. If nothing matches, it says so.

Real advice goes to real experts

Health, legal and money questions get pointed to a professional.

Your birth details are yours
